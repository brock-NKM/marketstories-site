Questions

Where do the numbers come from?

TRREB’s monthly Market Watch report. Every post with a figure carries the line “Source: TRREB Market Watch, [Month Year]”.

Will my title and brokerage be on every post?

Yes, exactly as registered. Ontario advertising rules ask for it, so it’s built into every layout.

Do I have to post everything?

No. You can skip or change any post, and nothing is used until you approve it.

How do I cancel?

Give us 7 days’ notice before your next billing date and you won’t be billed again. Email brock@marketstories.ca.
